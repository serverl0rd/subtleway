Put them where you want

Position it perfectly — right on screen

Subtitles covering the action? Move them. Subtleway lets you nudge captions with sliders or simply grab and drag them to the exact spot — then hit Save to lock it in and clear the guide.

  • Drag-to-place: a clear guide box appears over the captions; drop them anywhere.
  • Save / Done button right on the guide — and Esc to exit — so you're never stuck.
  • Vertical & horizontal sliders for fine, repeatable placement.
  • Works in fullscreen, following the video wherever it goes.

Like the player's own controls

A floating control that knows when to appear

Just like the play bar on Netflix and Prime, Subtleway's floating button fades in when you move the mouse or pause, and quietly fades away a few seconds after you resume. One click opens a compact on-screen panel.

Legitimately yours

Switch subtitle language in a click

On Netflix, Subtleway reads the subtitle tracks your account already provides — through the player's own API — and lets you switch between them instantly, without digging into the settings menu. On Prime Video, language is handled by the player's built-in subtitle menu.

  • Uses only the tracks your own subscription includes — nothing is fetched or injected.
  • No downloading, hosting, or sharing of subtitle files. Ever.
  • Instant switching from the popup or the on-screen panel.

⚖️ Legitimate subscriptions only

Subtleway works with your own legitimate subscription only. It restyles the subtitles a service already displays to you and switches between the subtitle tracks your account already includes. It does not enable, encourage, or support piracy, account sharing, or bypassing any paid service, and it does not download, host, or inject external subtitle content or defeat any content protection. Subtleway collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere — your preferences stay on your device.
