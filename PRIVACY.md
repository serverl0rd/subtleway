# Subtleway — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026_

Subtleway is built by **BuildCraft Labs** with privacy as a default, not a
feature.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Subtleway has no analytics, no telemetry, no accounts, and no
network requests of its own. It does not phone home.

## What is stored, and where

Your subtitle preferences (colour, font, size, position, and so on) are saved
**locally on your device** using the browser's `chrome.storage.local` API. This
data never leaves your computer and is only used to re-apply your chosen styling.

You can erase it at any time with **“Reset everything”** in the popup, or by
removing the extension.

## Permissions we request, and why

- **`storage`** — to remember your subtitle preferences on your device.
- **Host access to Netflix and Amazon / Prime Video** — so the content script
  can restyle the subtitles on those sites. Subtleway runs on no other sites.

## What Subtleway does not do

- It does not read, record, or transmit what you watch.
- It does not download, host, or inject any subtitle or video content.
- It does not bypass, weaken, or remove any content protection.
- It does not facilitate piracy or account sharing.
